## What this shows

At `limit=1`, p95 total is **3787 ms** of which **3756 ms is queueing** and only 32 ms is service. At `limit=128` the same work reports **29 ms**.

The service time never changed. It is a property of the work, not of the
client. Everything else was admission delay — and a single reported
latency number cannot tell you which of the two you are looking at.

That distinction decides the fix. Admission-bound means raise the limit
or shed load; service-bound means a faster backend. Acting on the wrong
one wastes the effort.

## Limitations

- **Simulated backend, not a live endpoint.** The claim under test is
  about this client's admission control; real API variance would swamp
  the effect. It does not predict latency against any real provider.
- Single machine, single event loop. No network, no TLS, no DNS.
- `asyncio.sleep` is a lower bound on real I/O: no connection pool
  contention, no head-of-line blocking from HTTP/2 multiplexing.
